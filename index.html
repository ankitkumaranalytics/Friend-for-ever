Dear Aashi,

I don't know exactly when it happened, but somewhere between conversations, little moments and the way you treat me, you became someone I genuinely care about.

On 03 - 10 - 2026, at 2:25 AM, I realized how deeply I cared about you. That feeling was mine to hold, and the memory still stays close to me.

I like your innocence.
I like the way you care.
I like the way you respect me.
I like the little playful side of you.

And I really like how, even when I'm not in the best mood, talking to you can somehow make me feel happy.

Last night, October 3 at 9:33 PM, you told me you didn't want a relationship and wanted us to be friends. We talked through the night, and at 9:02 this morning I said yes to friendship.

I won't pretend my feelings disappeared overnight. I want to care for you, respect you and be there when I can, without asking you to love me or give those feelings back. Your choice matters, and so does making room for both of us to feel comfortable and respected in this friendship.

Some questions from that night are still in my heart. Maybe it will take a long time to understand them. A quiet part of me will keep hoping for your love; right now, you feel like the last person my heart could choose. But that hope and those feelings are mine to carry—you don't owe me the same, and I won't ask you to promise me a future.

This yes came with a quiet ache, but also with care for what you asked for. I hope we can let friendship be honest, kind and freely chosen—without either of us having to hide who we are or promise more than we can give.

I'm grateful for the moments we've shared, and I'll carry them gently.

— Someone who genuinely cares ♡
